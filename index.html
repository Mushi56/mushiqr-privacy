Privacy Policy

How Mushi QR Pro protects your personal information, respects your privacy, and processes your data on Android. Last updated October 17, 2026.

The Short Version

Your privacy, summarized in plain English.

Creating QR codes, styling barcodes, and scanning live camera feeds happens 100% locally on your phone. If you choose to create an account, we store your user profile, Google Play subscription status, and any projects you explicitly choose to sync with Cloud Sync. We also count anonymous app visits to ensure network stability. We do not run ads, never sell your data, and never receive or store your payment card numbers.

1. Who We Are and What This Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Mushi QR Pro (Android application, version 1.1.10) collects, uses, and safeguards information. The application is solely engineered and operated by Mushtaq Ahmad (Buner, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan).

This policy applies to all features of the mobile application, including the QR code generator, barcode studio, batch creation tools, camera scanner, preset template library, Cloud Sync service, Google Play subscriptions, and account management.

2. What Stays on Your Device (100% Local Processing)

The core functionality of Mushi QR Pro runs strictly on-device. The following content and operations are never transmitted to our servers:

  • Generating QR Codes & Barcodes: All visual rendering, custom dot shapes, eye frames, color gradients, and layout templates are compiled locally in memory.
  • Logos and User Images: Cropping, transparency filters, color picking, and artistic photo blends are executed entirely on your hardware.
  • Camera & Gallery Scanning: Real-time camera viewfinder frames, zoom processing, and gallery image decoding are calculated locally. Camera frames are never stored or uploaded.
  • File Exports: Compiling PNG, JPG, vector SVG, PDF documents, and bulk ZIP bundles happens in local device storage.
  • Preferences & Settings: Theme selections (dark/light), sound effects, haptic vibrations, default folder paths, and preferred currency.
Local Storage Notice: Your recent 50 creations, saved presets, and local settings reside in your device's private storage. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app permanently erases this local data unless you have mirrored it to Cloud Sync.

3. Information We Collect (And Why)

3.1 Anonymous App Visits (Even Without an Account)

Upon launching the application, the app assigns a random pseudo-anonymous device identifier and records an anonymous visit entry: initial launch time, last active timestamp, total visit count, operating platform, device language, and screen dimensions. If you later create an account, this record is linked to your profile to prevent duplicate trials and ensure server health.

3.2 Account Information (Only If You Sign Up)

Creating an account is completely optional. If you sign in via Email or Google Sign-In, we collect:

  • Email address, display name, and avatar picture (provided via Google Sign-In or manual sign-up).
  • Account creation date, last sign-in timestamp, and sign-in provider type.
  • Passwords: If you use email authentication, your password is encrypted and managed directly by Firebase Authentication. We never see or hold your plaintext password.

3.3 Cloud Sync Data (Only When Signed In)

When signed in, projects you create or save can be synchronized to your private cloud storage. This includes encoded text (URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, vCard contact information) and styling parameters. You can delete cloud-synced projects and history at any time from your Account panel without affecting the local copy on your phone.

3.4 Subscription & Google Play Billing Data

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed directly by Google Play Billing. We receive:

  • Plan tier, renewal status, trial entitlement, expiration date, and transaction timestamps.
  • A cryptographically hashed Google Play purchase token used to prevent duplicate activation fraud.
  • RevenueCat entitlement identifiers to sync your Pro status across your devices.

✓ We never receive, process, or store your credit card, debit card, or bank account details.

3.5 Administrative Security Records

When administrative plan grants, role modifications, or configuration adjustments occur, an audit log records the timestamp and action for system integrity.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Device Data: Retained locally until you clear app storage or uninstall the app from your Android device.

Cloud Sync Data: Maintained while your account remains active. You can clear cloud projects directly in the app.

Google Play Account & Data Deletion Request

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer policies and global data protection standards (GDPR/CCPA), you have the absolute right to delete your account, synced cloud designs, and all personal records at any time.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)
Navigate to your Profile section (via the You tab profile card or by opening your Profile menu > My Profile > Delete Account). Confirming your decision will instantly and irreversibly erase your cloud profile, history, saved QR codes, presets, local device cache, and authentication credentials.
Option 2: Email Deletion Request
You can also email our developer support directly at mushi56.official@gmail.com from your registered account address with the subject "Delete My Account". We will complete your request within 30 days.

Important Note: Account deletion does not automatically cancel Google Play subscriptions. Please cancel any active recurring subscriptions directly through the Google Play Store app (Profile > Payments & subscriptions).
